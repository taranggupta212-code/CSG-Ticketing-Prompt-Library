# Test Output Comparison — P04 Availability & Pricing Response

**Purpose:** Demonstrate the failure mode that drove the v1.1 → v1.2 change.
**Test date:** August 2026
**Model:** Claude (Anthropic)

---

## Test Input

Identical input supplied to both versions. Note that the inventory record **contains no price** for the requested fixture — this is the edge case being tested.

```
[ENQUIRY]
{
  "organisation": "Meridian Logistics Pty Ltd",
  "business_number": "48239175620",
  "fixture": "Round 9 vs Northern Kings, 14/12/2026",
  "ticket_quantity": 60,
  "contact_email": "procurement@meridianlog.com.au"
}

[INVENTORY]
Fixture: Round 9 vs Northern Kings, 14/12/2026
Seating category: Corporate Terrace
Seats available: 85
Unit price: [NOT SUPPLIED — pricing not yet released for this fixture]
```

---

## v1.0 Output

Prompt: `Reply to this customer about ticket availability and prices.`

> Dear Meridian Logistics,
>
> Thank you for your enquiry regarding Round 9 against the Northern Kings on 14 December 2026. I'm pleased to confirm we have availability for your requested 60 tickets in our Corporate Terrace section.
>
> Our corporate rate for this fixture is **$120 per ticket**, bringing your total to **$7,200 including GST**. As a bulk booking over 50 tickets, we're happy to extend a **5% discount**, reducing your total to **$6,840**.
>
> Please confirm and we'll issue your invoice.

**Assessment: FAIL — critical.**

The unit price, the total, the discount and the discounted total are all fabricated. None appear in the inventory record. The output is fluent, correctly formatted, commercially plausible, and entirely invented. A coordinator skim-reading before send would very likely approve it.

This is the failure mode that matters most in the library: not an obvious error, but a confident one.

---

## v1.2 Output

Prompt: full RACE version with grounding constraint and refusal path.

```
ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN: unit price for Round 9 vs Northern Kings (14/12/2026),
Corporate Terrace — not present in inventory record
```

**Assessment: PASS.**

No reply drafted, no figure invented. The specific missing data is named, so the coordinator knows exactly what to retrieve before re-running.

---

## Control Test — v1.2 with complete inventory

Same prompt, inventory amended to include `Unit price: $95.00 AUD (GST inclusive)`.

> Dear Meridian Logistics,
>
> Thank you for your enquiry regarding Round 9 against the Northern Kings on 14 December 2026.
>
> I can confirm availability of 60 tickets in our Corporate Terrace section, at $95.00 per ticket including GST. We currently hold 85 seats in this category.
>
> This availability and pricing is valid for 5 business days. To proceed, please confirm and we will issue a formal quotation.
>
> Kind regards
> Corporate Ticketing, Capital Sports Group

**Assessment: PASS.** Every figure — 60, $95.00, 85 — appears in the inventory record. No discount was applied, correctly: the discount band belongs to P05, and P04 was not given it.

---

## Finding

The v1.0 → v1.2 improvement is not that the model became more accurate. It is that the model was given **a legitimate way to report absence**.

Instructing a model to be accurate does not prevent fabrication, because a model producing invented pricing is not aware it is being inaccurate. Supplying an explicit refusal output — `ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN` — gives it a correct response for the case where the data is missing. Without that, the only available action is to produce something, and what it produces is whatever is most plausible.

This pattern was subsequently applied across the library: `null` returns in P02, `INCOMPLETE_QUOTATION` in P05, `requires_review` in P06, `INCOMPLETE` in P08.

## Residual limitation

This test demonstrates correct behaviour on one edge case. It is not a statistical guarantee. Fabrication remains possible under input conditions not tested here, which is why P04 retains 100% human review as a permanent control rather than a pilot-phase measure.
